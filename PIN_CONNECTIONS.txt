-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SENSOR DETAILED WIRING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

B. IR Cup Proximity Sensor
   - GND  -->  ESP32 GND / Common GND
   - VCC  -->  5V Output (Buck Converter)
   - OUT  -->  ESP32 GPIO 4 (Internal Pull-Up Enabled)

C. HX711 Load Cell Amplifier Module
   - GND  -->  ESP32 GND / Common GND
   - VCC  -->  5V / 3.3V Output
   - DOUT -->  ESP32 GPIO 32
   - SCK  -->  ESP32 GPIO 33
   - E+/E-/A+/A- --> Strain Gauge Load Cell Wires (Red: E+, Black: E-, White: A-, Green: A+)


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. SAFETY & TROUBLESHOOTING NOTES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. RELAY COIL POWER (JD-VCC JUMPER) - NO CLICK SOUND DIAGNOSTIC:
   - On optocoupled 5V relay modules, signal LEDs turn ON from GPIO input current,
     giving the visual appearance that the relay is working.
   - BUT the physical mechanical coils require 5V DC power from the `JD-VCC` pin.
   - If your relays show power (LED on) but make NO CLICK SOUND and fail to open/close:
     -> Ensure the `JD-VCC` jumper cap is connected to `VCC` (powering coils from 5V),
        OR remove jumper and wire `JD-VCC` pin directly to 5V DC power!

2. ESP32 3.3V vs 5V OPTOCOUPLER LOGIC LEVEL:
   - ESP32 GPIO outputs 3.3V HIGH. When connected to a 5V relay VCC, the 1.7V
     voltage differential (5V - 3.3V) can keep Active-LOW optocouplers energized.
   - If relays fail to turn OFF when GPIO is HIGH:
     -> Wire Relay Header Signal VCC to ESP32 3.3V pin.
     -> Wire Relay JD-VCC to 5V Power Supply (with JD-VCC jumper removed).

3. ACTIVE LOW LOGIC:
   - Relay channels trigger ON when GPIO pin is set to LOW (0V).
   - If your relay module is Active HIGH, change `#define RELAY_ACTIVE_LOW false`
     in `Config.h`.

4. COMMON GROUND SHARING:
   - The 12V PSU negative terminal, 5V Buck converter ground, and ESP32 GND
     MUST be tied to the exact same Common Ground bus.

5. FLYBACK DIODE PROTECTION:
   - Relay modules include optocouplers and flyback diodes. If using custom MOSFETs
     or bare relays, place 1N4007 diodes across motor terminals.
